## INGREDIENT SUBSTITUTION GUIDE

### Protein Substitutions:
- **Chicken Breast** ↔ Chicken Thighs (cheaper, more flavor)
- **Chicken** ↔ Pork Shoulder (when on sale)
- **Pork** ↔ Ground Turkey (Walmart $3.48/lb)
- **Whole Eggs** ↔ Egg Whites (lower fat, higher protein ratio)
- **Tuna** ↔ Canned Chicken or Canned Salmon
- **Black Beans** ↔ Pinto Beans, Kidney Beans, or Chickpeas
- **Lentils** ↔ Split Peas or any canned beans

### Carb Substitutions:
- **White Rice** ↔ Brown Rice (more fiber, same cost)
- **Rice** ↔ Quinoa (higher protein, more expensive)
- **Rice** ↔ Sweet Potato (nutrient-dense)
- **Tortillas** ↔ Pita Bread or Rice Cakes
- **Oats** ↔ Cream of Wheat (Walmart $2.48/28oz)
- **Potatoes** ↔ Sweet Potatoes (more nutrients)
- **Bread** ↔ Rice Cakes (gluten-free, cheaper)

### Dairy Substitutions:
- **Cottage Cheese** ↔ Greek Yogurt (similar protein)
- **Cheddar Cheese** ↔ Mozzarella or Mexican Blend
- **Greek Yogurt** ↔ Regular Yogurt + Protein Powder (if allowed)
- **Milk** ↔ Water or Broth (user avoids milk)

### Vegetable Substitutions:
- **Frozen Mixed Vegetables** ↔ Any single frozen veggie (broccoli, peas, corn)
- **Fresh Spinach** ↔ Frozen Spinach (cheaper, lasts longer)
- **Fresh Tomatoes** ↔ Canned Diced Tomatoes
- **Fresh Onions** ↔ Frozen Diced Onions
- **Fresh Garlic** ↔ Garlic Powder (1 clove = ⅛ tsp powder)

### Fat Substitutions:
- **Olive Oil** ↔ Vegetable Oil (cheaper)
- **Peanut Butter** ↔ Almond Butter (more expensive) or Sunflower Seed Butter
- **Butter** ↔ Olive Oil or Coconut Oil

### Fruit Substitutions:
- **Banana** ↔ Apple, Orange, or Frozen Berries
- **Fresh Fruit** ↔ Canned Fruit in Water (cheaper, longer shelf life)
- **Fresh Berries** ↔ Frozen Berries (much cheaper)

## BUDGET-SAVING TIPS

### Buy in Bulk:
- **Rice** - Buy 5lb or 10lb bags (much cheaper per pound)
- **Oats** - Buy large containers (42oz or bigger)
- **Beans** - Buy dried beans instead of canned (⅓ the cost)
- **Chicken** - Buy family packs and freeze portions
- **Eggs** - Buy 18 or 24 count (cheaper per egg)

### Freeze for Later:
- **Burritos** - Make 20+ at once, freeze individually
- **Cooked Rice** - Freeze in portions, microwave to reheat
- **Cooked Beans** - Freeze in 1-cup portions
- **Bread/Tortillas** - Freeze to prevent waste
- **Chicken/Pork** - Buy on sale, freeze portions
- **Egg Muffins** - Bake batch, freeze, reheat as needed

### Shop Sales:
- **Pork Shoulder** - Stock up when under $2/lb
- **Chicken** - Buy when under $1.50/lb
- **Canned Goods** - Stock up during 10 for $10 sales
- **Frozen Vegetables** - Buy when on sale, they last months
- **Cheese** - Buy blocks on sale, shred yourself (cheaper)
