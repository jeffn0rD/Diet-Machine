## 🛒 SHOPPING TIPS

### Best Days to Shop:
- **Wednesday**: New sales start, fresh produce stocked
- **Sunday Evening**: Markdowns on meat/produce expiring soon
- **Early Morning**: Best selection, less crowded

### Store Layout Strategy:
1. **Start with Produce** - Pick freshest items first
2. **Frozen Section** - Grab frozen items early, they stay cold
3. **Meat Counter** - Check for manager's specials
4. **Dairy** - Last stop to keep cold items fresh
5. **Checkout** - Review receipt for errors

## 🎯 QUANTITY GUIDE FOR MEAL PREP

### How Much to Buy for 7 Days:

**Proteins:**
- Eggs: 24 count = 3-4 per day
- Chicken: 3 lb = 12-15 servings (4 oz each)
- Pork: 2 lb = 8-10 servings (3-4 oz each)
- Greek Yogurt: 32 oz = 4 servings (1 cup each)
- Cottage Cheese: 24 oz = 4 servings (½ cup each)

**Carbs:**
- Rice: 2 lb uncooked = 6 cups cooked = 12 servings
- Oats: 42 oz = 26 servings (40g each)
- Tortillas: 16 count = 2+ per day
- Lentils: 1 lb = 5 cups cooked = 10 servings

**Vegetables:**
- Frozen mixed: 2 bags = 14 servings (1 cup each)
- Spinach: 10 oz = 5-7 servings
- Tomatoes: 3 = 6 servings (½ each)
- Onions: 3 = 6-9 servings

**Fats:**
- Peanut butter: 16 oz = 16 servings (2 tbsp each)
- Olive oil: 17 oz = 34 servings (1 tbsp each)

## 💡 SUBSTITUTION SHOPPING GUIDE

### If Item Unavailable or Too Expensive:

**Chicken Breast** → Chicken Thighs (cheaper, more flavor)
**Pork Shoulder** → Ground Turkey or Chicken Thighs
**Greek Yogurt** → Regular Yogurt + Cottage Cheese
**Fresh Spinach** → Frozen Spinach (cheaper, lasts longer)
**Cheddar Cheese** → Mozzarella or Mexican Blend
**Bananas** → Apples or Frozen Berries
**Black Beans** → Pinto Beans or Kidney Beans
**Olive Oil** → Vegetable Oil (much cheaper)
**Tortillas** → Bread or Pita
**Fresh Tomatoes** → Canned Diced Tomatoes
