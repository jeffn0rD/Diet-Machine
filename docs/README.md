## 📅 Meal Prep Strategy

### Sunday Batch Cooking (2-3 hours)
1. **Cook Proteins (60 min):** Chicken, pork, hard-boiled eggs
2. **Cook Carbs (30 min):** Rice, lentils
3. **Prep Vegetables (20 min):** Wash, chop, portion
4. **Assemble Burritos (45 min):** 20-30 burritos for the week
5. **Prep Snacks (15 min):** Portion containers
6. **Storage & Labeling (15 min):** Organize fridge/freezer

### Daily Quick Assembly (5-10 minutes)
- Microwave frozen burritos OR
- Assemble bowls with pre-cooked components OR
- Quick scrambles and stir-fries

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Budget Optimization:
1. **Shop Wednesday:** New sales start, fresh produce stocked
2. **Use Walmart App:** Digital coupons save $5-10/week
3. **Buy Generic:** Great Value brand saves 20-30%
4. **Stock Up on Sales:** Chicken <$2/lb, pork <$2/lb
5. **Freeze Everything:** Prevents waste, saves $10-15/week

### Meal Prep Success:
1. **Start Small:** Master 3-4 meals before expanding
2. **Batch Cook:** Make 20+ burritos at once
3. **Label Everything:** Contents and date on all containers
4. **Freeze Smart:** Portion before freezing for easy use
5. **Prep Snacks:** Pre-portion for grab-and-go convenience

### Protein Goals:
1. **Every Meal Counts:** Aim for 25-50g per main meal
2. **Snack Smart:** Choose high-protein snacks (eggs, yogurt, cheese)
3. **Double Up:** Add eggs to oatmeal, beans to rice
4. **Track Week 1:** Understand your portions and patterns
5. **Adjust as Needed:** More protein if still hungry

## 📞 Support & Adjustments

### If You Need to Adjust:
- **Gaining Weight:** Reduce rice/carb portions by 25%
- **Losing Weight:** Increase protein portions, add snacks
- **Still Hungry:** Add more vegetables (low calorie, filling)
- **Budget Too High:** Focus on essential items list ($73.60)
- **Too Much Prep:** Start with just burritos and hard-boiled eggs

### Customization Options:
- All meals can be scaled up/down
- Substitute ingredients based on preferences
- Adjust spice levels to taste
- Add/remove vegetables as desired
- Modify cooking methods based on equipment
